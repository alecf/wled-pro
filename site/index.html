WLED Pro

A modern, mobile-first app for WLED LED controllers. Build light shows, carve your strips into zones, and browse effects and palettes from your phone.

Get started View on GitHub

Free and open source. Runs in your browser; installs as an app.

Light shows, visually

See every segment of a show as a strip. Tap one to change its effect, colors, palette, speed and LED range. Split, merge and name segments, preview edits live on the device, and Cancel puts everything back.

Zones you define once

Describe your physical layout ("Couch", "TV Wall", "Window"), grouped by room. Apply it to any show to split it into matching WLED segments. The layout is stored on the controller, so every phone sees it.

Only the controls that matter

WLED Pro reads each effect's metadata, so the editor shows just the colors, sliders and options that effect uses, with real palette previews from your device.

Settings without the hunt

WiFi, LED hardware and power budget, time and location, sync, security, schedules, playlists and the sleep timer, in one place. Switch between all your controllers in a tap.

Made for your phone

Built on WLED

WLED Pro is only a front end. Everything that makes your lights shine (the effects engine, palettes, segments, presets, playlists, sync and the JSON API this app talks to) is WLED, the open source firmware for ESP8266 and ESP32 created by Aircoookie (Christian Schwinne) and built by a large community of contributors.

Thank you to everyone who has written code, effects, documentation and answers for WLED. If it lights up your home, consider supporting the project.

Get started

You need a WLED controller on your network and Bun. Serve WLED Pro over HTTP on your local network, since WLED controllers only speak HTTP. 

git clone https://github.com/alecf/wled-pro.git
cd wled-pro
bun install
bun run dev --host

Open the address it prints on your phone, tap Add Controller, and enter your controller's IP address or hostname.
